# 🛍️ KiranaSaathi

### AI Decision Intelligence for Small Businesses

> **Turn everyday business activity into a living financial model — and make better decisions before risking real money.**

KiranaSaathi is a multilingual AI-powered decision platform designed for small Indian retailers and Kirana businesses.

Instead of simply recording transactions, KiranaSaathi transforms everyday business activity into a **Merchant Financial Twin** that helps merchants understand their current financial position, evaluate risks, and simulate business decisions before taking action.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Small merchants manage sales, expenses, supplier payments, customer credit and inventory through a combination of:

- Memory
- Paper notebooks
- Informal records
- Manual calculations
- Fragmented digital tools

This makes it difficult to answer important business questions:

> **"Can I afford ₹50,000 more inventory this week?"**

> **"Why is my cash balance falling?"**

> **"What happens if I increase my stock purchase?"**

> **"Can I handle my upcoming supplier payments?"**

The problem is not a lack of business activity.

### **The problem is turning that activity into reliable, usable financial intelligence.**

---

# 💡 Our Solution

### **KiranaSaathi**

KiranaSaathi allows merchants to record business activity naturally through **voice, text or manual input**, including local-language and code-mixed interactions.

The system converts these inputs into verified financial transactions, continuously updates a **Merchant Financial Twin**, and uses that financial state to provide explainable insights and simulate hypothetical business decisions.

### Core flow

**Capture → Verify → Understand → Simulate → Decide**

---

# 🚀 What Makes KiranaSaathi Different?

Most business tools focus on:

> **"What happened?"**

KiranaSaathi focuses on:

> **"What is happening, what could happen, and what happens if I make this decision?"**

### 1. 🧠 Merchant Financial Twin

A continuously updated computational representation of the merchant's business.

It can represent:

- Cash position
- Sales
- Expenses
- Customer receivables
- Supplier payables
- Inventory
- Profit indicators
- Cash-flow stability

The Financial Twin is recalculated from verified transaction data.

---

### 2. 🎙️ Natural & Multilingual Input

Merchants can communicate naturally instead of navigating complex accounting forms.

Example:

> **"Aaj ₹8,500 sale hua, Ramesh ko ₹500 udhaar diya aur Sharma supplier ko ₹3,000 payment kiya."**

KiranaSaathi interprets the statement and converts it into structured business events.

---

### 3. ✅ Confidence-Aware Verification

AI does not directly write financial information into the ledger.
